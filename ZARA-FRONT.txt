THE 3 CALLS

1. Page opens
GET /api/vitrine
You fill the piece dropdown.
You show how many packs are left today: the number "reste".

2. User clicks "Faire les 5 photos"
POST /api/pack?sku=cabas-cognac&fond=F1
Body = the photo file, raw bytes.
You replace the center with the 5 image URLs from the response.
All 5 stay on the same background.

3. User clicks "Mots pour le site"
POST /api/fiche
Send sku, couleur, prix, matiere, taille.
Only if prix is a number in euros. If not, show: Prix en euros manquant.
You display title, meta and alts. You do not write that text.

WHO WEARS IT
The field "scene" decides. You do not decide.
scene = modele means clothes or shoes. Show: Yazid, Nour, Leila, Lina.
scene = table means a bag or an object. Show: Table, Pierre, Atelier.
Never show a person for a bag, a box or a bowl.

VIEWS
Bag: face, three-quarter, material, side, handles.

Text: Limite du jour atteinte.
